Millions of people shop on Amazon.com. It is the largest online store in the U.S.A. The founder and leader of the company is Jeff Bezos.

Bezos’ love for computers started at a young age. He enjoyed doing science projects during his childhood. Bezos pursued this interest as he grew up. In college, he studied engineering and computer science.

After graduation, Bezos developed computer software at several companies. At the last one, he became the vice president. He was the youngest person to hold that position.

In 1994, Bezos quit his job to start his own company. He wanted to be part of the growing online business. People thought he was making a mistake, but he was confident. Bezos made a business plan and hired people to start the company. Together, they set up Amazon.com in his garage.

Amazon.com was launched in 1995. It started as an online bookstore, selling books in the U.S.A. and other countries. Then, Bezos and his employees came up with more innovative ideas. They started selling different items, like CDs, clothes, and toys. Amazon.com quickly became very successful.

After 20 years, Amazon.com still continues to be a leader in online business. With Bezos in charge, Amazon.com has changed the way people shop.

pizza ever made or the richest cat? Interesting facts like these can be found in Guinness World Records.

Guinness World Records is a reference book. It has around 4,000 facts on all kinds of records. The book is popular with all ages. A) It even has its own record. B) No other annual book has ever sold more copies. C) Over 130 million copies have been sold so far! D) This is not a high figure compared to the sales volume of reference books.

Guinness World Records came from an argument about the fastest bird in Europe. Many people discussed it, but they couldn’t agree on the answer. They couldn’t find it in any book, either. This gave one of the people the idea to publish a book

about records. The first edition was published in Britain in 1955. Today, people around the world can read it in more than 25 languages.

The goal of Guinness World Records is to record great and unique achievements. The book celebrates these accomplishments. It also inspires people to break the records. Many people challenge themselves to become the best in the world in different areas.

Each year, Guinness World Records is updated with all the new records. The next time you get into an argument about a world record, you’ll know where to look. This book has the answer.
