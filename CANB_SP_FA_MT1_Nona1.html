Fingerprints are special patterns on fingers. They are often used to identify a person. Who started using them first? It was a man named William Herschel.

In 1858, William was in charge of building roads in India. He had workers write their names on their contracts. One day, a man came to make a contract. Suddenly, William got an idea. He asked the man to put down his handprint on the contract

instead of his name. This was the first time William took a person’s handprint.

William began to collect people’s handprints. He studied them and found out something interesting.

He didn’t need whole handprints to identify people after all. He only needed

fingerprints. That’s because fingerprints were unique to everybody. They didn’t change over time.

In 1877, William started using fingerprints for his new job. He had to give out money to old and sick people. Before, some people would collect the money using other people’s names. He used fingerprints to prevent those people from taking someone else’s money. After William, more and more people started using fingerprints

U1 L2

These days, fingerprints are very useful. We use fingerprints for many different purposes across diverse fields.

Police use fingerprints to solve cases. They keep a record of people’s fingerprints. At crime scenes, they look for fingerprints and study them. By doing this, police can identify criminals and catch them. They can also use fingerprints to find missing people.

Fingerprints are used in computers and smartphones for security. Some of them have fingerprint sensors that can identify their users. With these sensors, people can stop others from using their devices and keep their data safe.

Many schools use fingerprints to save time for students. By using fingerprint sensors, students don’t have to wait in long lines anymore. All they need to do is put their fingers on a sensor so that it can identify them. Then, they can buy their lunches or borrow books more quickly and easily.

Although there are different ways to identify people, fingerprints are still one of the best

ways today. That’s why they are used in so many places. Now, look at your fingerprints

and your friend’s. How do they compare? How different are they from one another?

In the past, people used the sun to tell the time. They set the clocks to noon when the sun was highest in the sky. This made the time different from city to city. For example, London and Oxford are two cities in Britain. London is to the east. The sun reaches its highest point there sooner than in Oxford. That made the time __________ in the two cities.

Having different local times became a problem when the railways expanded. Schedules were difficult to follow. Keeping trains on time was hard, so people often missed their trains. Britain wanted to fix this problem.

In the 1840s, railways started using one standard time. All the stations in Britain used Greenwich Time. They chose Greenwich because it had a famous clock. Then, train schedules became easy to follow.

Greenwich Time became the standard time throughout Britain in 1880. All cities used the same time. The time didn’t change when people traveled to different cities. Other countries liked this idea. They also wanted to use standard times.
