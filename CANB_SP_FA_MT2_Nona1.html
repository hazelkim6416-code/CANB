Reading · Unit 3 Lesson 1
Do you like to observe people? The waiting room in a doctor’s office is a great place to do this. Dr. Friedman and Dr. Rosenman observed their patients in the waiting room for over nine years. From this, they found two types of personalities.

Some people have a hard time sitting in one place. They cannot sit for long. They often stand up to walk around. These people are not good at waiting. They need to get a lot of things done. People like this are called Type A. Type A people are ambitious. They work very hard. They are also scared of failing.

The other group of people is quite different. They can sit and wait for a long time. They are happy doing just one thing. This group of people is called Type B. Type B people are easygoing. They are less worried about failing. They can relax more easily.

By observing their patients, Dr. Friedman and Dr. Rosenman learned a lot. Next, they wanted to find out how these two personality types affect people’s lives.

Reading · Unit 3 Lesson 2
While studying two types of personalities, Dr. Friedman and Dr. Rosenman learned that personality affects a person’s health and lifestyle.

The doctors first looked at their patients’ health. They learned that Type B people are healthier than Type A people. One reason is stress. Type A people easily get stressed. Too much stress is bad for the heart. For this reason, Type A people have more heart disease. Type B people are easygoing. They have less stress, so they have less heart disease.

Then, the doctors observed their patients’ lifestyles. They found that Type B people are better friends than Type A people. Type A people are ambitious. They work harder to succeed. They want their work done quickly and perfectly. Also, they easily get impatient and angry with others. This makes other people uncomfortable. However, Type B people get along more easily with others. They have better manners. People feel more comfortable around them.

The doctors’ studies showed how behavior, health, and lifestyle are different for each personality type. Think about the two personality types. Which one are you? Which one do you think is better?

Reading · Unit 4 Lesson 1
Blake looked at the clock and realized that it was already past midnight. He was still up. He was too stressed about his final exam.

After getting almost no sleep, Blake woke up feeling exhausted. His mom made him his favorite breakfast. He didn’t feel like eating, though, because he was under a lot of stress. He couldn’t enjoy the blueberry pancakes as he usually does.

When he arrived in the classroom, other students were chatting with each other. Blake didn’t join them because he was too nervous. He couldn’t stop thinking about how unprepared he felt. The teacher passed out the test, and Blake looked at the questions. He couldn’t understand any of them because his heart was pounding rapidly. He closed his eyes and breathed deeply because he had to focus and complete the test.

After taking the test, Blake walked out of the classroom. Just then, he remembered he didn’t put his name on the test. “How could I forget that? It’s all because of stress!” Blake felt helpless, but he couldn’t go back in time. For now, he has to find some ways to get rid of his stress and relax.
