Dear Abby,

My name is Jeff. I’m a student. I had a big fight with my best friend, Sam. One day, we planned to walk home together. But I forgot! So, Sam had to stay after school alone. Now, he doesn’t talk to me. How can we be good friends again?

Please help!

Dear Jeff,

Say sorry first. I know it’s hard. But you should work up your courage and talk to him. Or are you shy? Here’s an idea: write a sorry note and fold a paper animal. Give them to your friend, Sam. It always works.

your family!

You talked about eating in your country. Does everyone eat together in the U.S.A.? It’s different here in Nigeria.

What do you think of our eating culture?

Your friend,

Mariama
