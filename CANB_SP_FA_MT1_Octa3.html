U1 L1

Lucy was lonely. None of her friends lived close by. Her neighbor, Dave, was her age, but he was mean. When he saw Lucy, he never greeted her.

One day, Lucy wanted to ride her bike, but it had a flat tire. She didn't know how to pump up the tire. Dave was looking out of his window. When Lucy looked up, he looked away. She tried to pump up the tire, but it was hard for her. She really needed help. She thought Dave might help her. So she decided to be brave. She knocked on his door.

Dave opened the door, but he didn't say hello.

"Can you pump up my tire?" Lucy asked.

"Y...yes...," Dave answered shyly.

Dave and Lucy rode their bikes together and became friends. Now, Lucy knows Dave is not mean. He is just shy.

My family moved to a new neighborhood. Yesterday, I went outside to play soccer. I felt lonely because I didn’t have any friends, yet. But then, I met Randy. He lived close by. He was riding his bike. I was happy and waved at him. But he looked away and just passed by. I thought he was so mean! I felt bad. It suddenly started raining, and I came back home.

Then, someone knocked on my front door. It was Randy. He said, “Sorry, I didn’t greet you nicely. I had a flat tire and had to pump it up. Can we play together?”

We played board games in my house because it was still raining. When the weather gets better, we will play soccer together. Now, I know Randy is not mean. I’m so happy to have a friend in my new neighborhood!

U2 L1

Platypuses look like a duck, a beaver, and an otter. They have a duck's bill and webbed feet. They also have a beaver's tail and an otter's fur. Some scientists didn't believe they were real when they saw pictures of them.

Platypuses are mammals, but they lay eggs. The eggs hatch in about ten days. The mothers take care of their babies until they can swim alone.

On Land

∙ Platypuses live on land near water.

∙ They hide the webs on their feet when they walk or run.

∙ They use their feet to make holes for shelter.

∙ They lay eggs in their shelter.

In Water

∙ Platypuses hunt underwater.

∙ They use their bill to find food.

∙ They store the food in a special pouch inside their mouth.

∙ They use their webbed feet and tail to swim.

∙ Skin covers their eyes and ears. So water can't enter.

∙ They can stay underwater for two minutes.
