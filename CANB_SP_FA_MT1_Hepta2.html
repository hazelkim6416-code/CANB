U2

Insects are very small.

They have many enemies.

They have to protect themselves.

Some insects fight.

Bees can sting.

They protect themselves by fighting.

Some insects hide.

Leaf insects look like a leaf.

They protect themselves by hiding.

Some insects have body armor.

Beetles have hard shells.

They protect themselves with body armor.

Some insects smell.

Stink bugs have a bad smell.

They protect themselves by smelling bad.

Insects protect themselves in many ways.

But many insects just run or fly away.
