Reading · Unit 3 Lesson 1
People have always loved gazing at stars at night. Now, besides stars, there are some interesting things to spot in the sky. They are artificial satellites, and the International Space Station (ISS) is one of them.

The ISS is the largest artificial satellite in space. It is about the size of a football field and weighs about 419,000 kilograms. There are more than 100 rooms for controlling systems and research labs. Usually six astronauts live there carrying out experiments and studying space.

Unlike other satellites, the ISS was built outside the Earth. Because of its size, its parts were sent piece by piece to space and put together. The first piece was sent in 1998. It took 13 years and 115 space flights for the ISS to be fully completed in 2011.

For something so big, the ISS moves very fast. It only takes 90 minutes to orbit the Earth. This means the ISS orbits the Earth 16 times in a 24-hour period. This allows astronauts on the ISS to watch 16 sunrises and sunsets in a single day.

The ISS serves as a space airport. Space vehicles bring astronauts and space tourists to the station. Since 1998, these vehicles have carried more than 200 astronauts in over 360 flights. Space tourists have been flying there since 2001.

The ISS would be an interesting place to visit. __________________________________ These experiments help people better understand what it would be like to live in space.

Space is unlike the Earth. There is no air to breathe, soil to stand on, or water to drink. Astronauts on the International Space Station (ISS) conduct experiments to see if different living things can ________ in space.

One of the experiments is to find out whether animals can adapt to living on the ISS. A group of mice survived on the ISS longer than any other animal. They lived for three months, which is like seven human years for mice. Studies on animals show how well humans can survive on the ISS.

Another experiment is growing plants. Astronauts have successfully grown vegetables by using a plant growth system called Veggie. It provides vegetables with everything they need to grow. Having access to fresh vegetables helps humans stay healthy on the ISS.

Astronauts also conduct experiments to examine how well bacteria survive. A study demonstrated that bacteria become stronger on the ISS, and this is not good news for humans. Bacteria can make the air and the food on the ISS go bad, which will eventually cause humans to become sick. Astronauts are working on ways to fight bacteria on the ISS.

Do humans have a future in space? The astronauts on the ISS are trying to answer this question.
