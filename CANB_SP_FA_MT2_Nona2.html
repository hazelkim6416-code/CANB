Some animals are born with jobs. Ants, for example, have special jobs from birth. In a colony, they are divided into four kinds depending on their roles.

First, there are queen ants. Queen ants are females. They are the biggest ants, and they are born with wings. Queen ants found colonies. When they become old enough to produce young, they fly away to mate. Then, they lay thousands of eggs to establish their own colonies.

Drone ants are another kind. They are the only males in the colony and have small heads, big eyes, and wings to fly. Mating with queen ants is their only job. When the time is right, they fly away to mate with queens in the air.

Then, there are worker ants. They are females like queen ants. Worker ants are the tiniest, but they are actually very helpful for their colony. They do most of the chores. They feed and look after other ants. They also build and fix the nest.

Each type of ant is given a specific job by nature. What other animals have special jobs?

Reading · Unit 3 Lesson 2
Nature gives many animals their jobs. However, this is not always the case. Sometimes, animals are “hired” by people to do special jobs.

Dolphins are excellent divers. They can stay underwater for a long time, and they can also see and hear things well in the deep, dark sea. That is why the navy hires dolphins. Dolphins are trained to detect underwater bombs and enemy divers.

Dogs work for the police. Because of their incredible sense of smell, they are great at tracking. In fact, they can smell 100,000 times better than people. They can smell the traces of things that were left a week before. This is why they are trained to find missing people and criminals. They can also smell hidden items. So, they are even tasked with searching for dangerous items such as drugs and bombs.

Do you know who hires goats? Airports do! Goats are amazing gardeners for airports because they can eat large areas of grass quickly. They are hired to mow the grassy areas around the runway by eating the grass, which helps airports keep their runways clean and well-maintained.

Some animals are born with jobs while others are hired by people. Regardless of how they get their jobs, all animals live busy lives doing their work.

One March day in Dallas, Tommy woke up surprised as he saw something unexpected outside. Everything was completely covered in snow. He rushed downstairs with his brother, Nicky.

“Mom! Look outside! Isn’t it amazing that we’re having snow in March?” Tommy asked Mom excitedly. “What I heard from the news is that we haven’t had this much snowfall in March in over 60 years.” Mom smiled and said, “All the schools are closed today!” “Yeah!” the boys yelled and gave each other high fives.

After breakfast, they swept the snow off of Dad’s car so that Dad could get ready for work. However, the car wouldn’t start, so he took the bus instead. After Dad was gone, the boys played in the snow all day.

In the late afternoon, Mom left to get some groceries for dinner but came home empty-handed. “All the stores are closed, and all we have left are sausages and marshmallows,” she said.

A little later, when Dad got home, they found that the power had gone out, and their stove wasn’t working. Suddenly, Tommy exclaimed, “Let’s pretend we’re camping!”

“That’s a brilliant idea!” said Dad, and he made a fire in the fireplace. Then, the family roasted sausages and marshmallows over the fire and enjoyed the delicious food. In the end, everyone had such a good time that Tommy even wished every day was a snow day.
